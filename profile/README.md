# Research Center for Clinical Neuroimmunology and Neuroscience Basel

We work to improve the lives of people with multiple sclerosis and other neurological diseases. Our research turns clinical data, imaging, biomarkers and digital tools into better diagnosis, monitoring and treatment.

RC2NB is based at the University Hospital Basel (USB) and works closely with the University of Basel and the Swiss MS Cohort (SMSC).

## What you find here

This organisation hosts the software we build for our research:

- Tools for collecting and managing clinical and research data
- Digital biomarkers and assessment tools
- Analysis pipelines and shared libraries

Some repositories are public. Most are private while they are in development.

## How we work

- **Data protection first.** No patient data lives in any repository, public or private.
- **Quality by design.** We develop under a quality management system, with reviewed changes and documented releases.
- **Open where we can.** We publish code when it helps the research community and the licence allows it.
